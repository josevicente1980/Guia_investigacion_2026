---
name: compact-manuscript
description: Govern narrowly authorized manuscript reductions while preserving academic rigor.
---

The general compacting work is CLOSED. Do not merge or remove chapters, perform broad pruning, or delete substantive theory. Further reductions require explicit authorization and must be surgical: redundant outputs, oversized inspections, valueless graphics, repeated tables, or unproductive whitespace only.

Preserve causal cautions, assumptions, limitations, unit of analysis, weights, aggregation decisions, traceability, reproducibility, and central formulas. Keep necessary citations with claims. Never optimize to a page target by deleting essential scientific content.
