---
name: figures-tables-editorial
description: Inventory and audit manuscript figures and tables for editorial quality and institutional compliance.
---

Before editing, inventory the affected figures/tables and check numbering, captions, source, prior in-text mention, width, placement, duplication, and whether code output is incorrectly presented as a table. Apply institutional requirements only from official sources. Render and audit the result when required. Do not make global fixes before completing an inventory; do not alter unrelated scientific content.
