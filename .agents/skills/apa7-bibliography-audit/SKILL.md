---
name: apa7-bibliography-audit
description: Audit in-text citations and BibTeX references for APA 7 consistency and correspondence.
---

Check citations without references and uncited references, DOI and URL validity/format, author names, years, duplicates, titles, journal/book fields, capitalization, CSL-controlled italics, and BibTeX consistency. Verify sources before changing records. Do not remove sources solely because they are old; retain relevant foundational, methodological, normative, and historical works. Do not edit the bibliography unless authorized.
