---
name: docx-institutional-format
description: Safely adapt and verify Word reference documents and institutional page layout.
---

Target format: 170 mm wide × 240 mm high, portrait, 25 mm margins on all four sides.

Never overwrite `plantillas/plantilla.docx` during experiments; work on a copy. Prefer Word COM when available. Avoid broad OOXML rewrites. Verify saved dimensions and orientation in Word, then render and visually audit when the task calls for it. Measure pagination in Word. Do not change typography, captions, styles, or other layout properties unless explicitly authorized.
