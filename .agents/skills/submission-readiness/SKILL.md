---
name: submission-readiness
description: Assess whether the complete manuscript and administrative package meet UNL submission requirements.
---

Review current editorial rules, structure, submission dossier, figures, tables, APA, INEN, TOC, final files, administrative identifiers and documents, the applicable checklist, and similarity checks when applicable. Classify each finding PASS, REVIEW, or BLOCK. Never declare SUBMISSION READY while any critical BLOCK remains. Distinguish verified evidence from unresolved items and leave author decisions to the authors.
